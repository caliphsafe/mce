A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Before the
confetti.

Can you work with my theme and colors?

Share your inspiration, favorite colors, and the occasion in your inquiry. We’ll discuss how to make the details feel cohesive and personal.

Can I request just a backdrop or balloon arrangement?

Yes. Tell us whether you’re looking for one statement piece or several elements. We’ll discuss the options for your event.

How do I check availability and pricing?

Send your preferred date, location, and an idea of the setup you have in mind. Availability and pricing are discussed for your specific event; an inquiry does not reserve a date.

What should I include in my inquiry?

Your occasion, date, venue or city, colors, estimated budget, and the services you’re interested in are a great start. You can share inspiration photos when you contact us.
